Home Depot Debt and Credit Risk Analysis

Intermediate Accounting 2 · Group project (4 members) · Presented the credit rating and ratio analysis

Analyzed Home Depot's financing liabilities using its FY2025 annual report and SEC filings, then judged whether its credit rating matches its financial health.

  • Compared short-term and long-term debt year over year: total debt rose 4.5% to $55.8 billion, with more of it coming due within a year.
  • Calculated FFO / Total Debt (31.7%) and Debt / EBITDA (2.24x).
  • Concluded the ratios support the company's S&P "A" investment-grade rating.

Policy Memo: Federal Funding for Electric School Buses

Policy memo · Individual project · April 2026

Wrote an executive memo advising the President on whether to fund a phased switch from diesel to electric school buses, with priority for high-need districts.

  • Weighed upfront costs ($300,000 to $400,000 per bus, plus charging equipment) against health, attendance, and test-score benefits.
  • Compared three options: keep the current program, fund every district equally, or fund in targeted phases.
  • Recommended a three-phase, six-year rollout starting with $2 billion for the 500 highest-need districts, and included a SWOT analysis.
